# Red Flags

Promotion should usually be blocked when:
- performance disappears after modest costs
- pnl is dominated by a handful of dates, names, or trades
- the historical universe is built from current constituents
- the signal cannot be regenerated from point-in-time inputs
- a huge parameter sweep was used with weak economic reasoning
- the selection history is unknown, unbounded, or reconstructed from memory
- the strongest null story has no written answer
- returns are mostly explained by a cheap known factor once decomposed
- gross and net results are confused or inconsistently reported
- the implementation cannot be rerun end-to-end from raw data
- exposure or capacity limits are explained away instead of measured
- the promotion case never states sizing or marginal contribution to the book
- iterate decisions keep recurring with no budget and no new type of evidence
