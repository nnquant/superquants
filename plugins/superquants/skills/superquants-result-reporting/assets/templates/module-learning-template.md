### Failure Learning Evidence

Use this module for a non-pass result or material surprise:

- experiment result: pass, fail (valid negative), invalid experiment, or inconclusive, tied to the pre-committed criterion
- follow-up decision: promote, archive, debug, iterate, or gather evidence, recorded separately from the result
- prediction versus observation and the important gap
- earliest failing layer and the evidence for the leading cause versus alternatives
- what the result falsifies or weakens, and what remains untested
- reusable learning: the specific assumption, test, or gate that should change
- justified follow-up: the smallest next action with expected information gain, stop condition, and trial-counting consequence, or archive with no further experiment

`Fail` may be the final experiment conclusion, but do not stop the closeout at that one word. Do not turn an invalid run into evidence against the alpha thesis or relabel a valid fail to soften the evidence.
